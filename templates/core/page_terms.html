A university projectAstonBundle is a fictional shop built for the DG4AGA module at Aston University. Nothing here is a real offer to sell. No money is taken and nothing is delivered.

1. Who can use AstonBundle

Accounts are for Aston University students with an @aston.ac.uk email address. Please use test details only.

2. Prices and payment

Prices are shown in pounds sterling (GBP) and include VAT. Home-currency amounts are estimates for guidance only. Checkout is a simulation: you will never be asked for card details.

3. Delivery

You choose a delivery slot at your halls reception on one of the 3 days before you arrive, or on your arrival day. Delivery is free on orders over £{{ free_delivery_threshold }}.

4. Cancelling and returns

You can cancel an order online until we start processing it. Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery — see the returns policy.

5. Reviews

Only students who received an item can review it. Please keep reviews honest and polite, and do not include personal details. We may hide reviews that break these rules.

6. Your data

See our privacy notice. You can delete your account at any time.
