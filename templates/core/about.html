About us

We arrived to an empty room. You don’t have to.

AstonBundle is built by Group 2 — five Aston students. Many new students land late at night to a bare mattress, no kettle and the wrong plug. We want to fix that first night.

{% include "partials/ill_room.html" %}

The problem

New students land after long flights, often at night. Shops are closed, everything is unfamiliar, and buying basics in your first days costs time, money and stress.

Our idea

Order before you fly. We pack your essentials and deliver them to your halls reception on the date you choose — so your room is ready when you are.

Our promise

Fair prices in pounds, simple English, easy returns, and only the things you really need. No account data we don’t need.

How we work

Doing this responsibly

Fewer delivery trips

Orders for the same halls are grouped into delivery slots, so one van run serves many students instead of many separate parcels.

Less waste

Bundles contain only what a first-year room needs. Items come in reusable bags, and returned items go back into stock when they are unused.

Minimal personal data

We keep only your name, Aston email, halls and room. Staff see customer details masked, and every reveal is logged. No card details are ever collected.

Easy for everyone

Plain English, a legible typeface, prices in pounds with a home-currency estimate, and pages tested against WCAG 2.1 AA. Accessibility statement

Our AI suggestions use order history only, never personal details, and are always labelled. How suggestions work
