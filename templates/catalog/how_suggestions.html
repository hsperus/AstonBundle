How “Frequently bought together” works

We look at past orders and find products that students often buy in the same order. If many people who buy a kettle also buy a plate set — more often than chance — we suggest the plate set next to the kettle.

What we use

  • Which products appear together in orders.
  • Nothing about you personally: no name, email, address, course or country.
  • Your own basket, only to choose suggestions for this visit.

What we never do

  • We never suggest items that are out of stock.
  • Suggestions never change prices.
  • Suggestions are always labelled “Smart suggestion”.
