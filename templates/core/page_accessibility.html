We want everyone to be able to use AstonBundle, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or a phone. We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we have done

  • Text and controls meet AA colour contrast.
  • Every interactive element has a visible focus ring and works with a keyboard.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44 × 44 pixels.
  • Stock and order status always use an icon and text, never colour alone.
  • Form errors are shown next to the field in plain English.
  • The body font, Atkinson Hyperlegible, was designed for legibility.
  • Pages work from 320 px wide and at 200% zoom, and respect “reduce motion”.

Known limitations

  • The forecast chart in the staff area has a table alternative, but the chart itself is not fully described.
  • Product images are illustrations, not photos.

Tell us about a problem

If something does not work for you, please contact us.
