Suggested reorder = top of the 80% range − stock available now − stock already on order. You can edit the number before creating the order.

Model card

Problem
Avoid running out of key items before an intake, and avoid over-ordering after it.
Method
Gradient-boosted regression trees (scikit-learn), one model per quantile (10th, 50th, 90th). Direct multi-horizon: each row predicts week t+h from data known at week t. The target is normalised by last year’s units × recent growth, so the model can follow a growing shop.
Inputs
Weekly units per product, horizon, week of year, weeks to the next intake, same week last year, recent 4-week sales, growth vs last year, new verified sign-ups, price, bundle membership, category. No personal data.
Outputs
Units per product per week with a range; total for the intake period; suggested reorder.
Evaluation
Back-test on {{ metrics.period }} (trained only on earlier data). MAE per product: {{ metrics.mae }} units vs {{ metrics.baseline_mae }} for the baseline “same weeks last year”. WAPE {% widthratio metrics.wape 1 100 %}% vs {% widthratio metrics.baseline_wape 1 100 %}%.
80% range
Calibrated from back-test errors (×{{ metrics.range_low_factor }} to ×{{ metrics.range_high_factor }} of the median). Coverage {% widthratio metrics.range_coverage 1 100 %}% — in-sample, so expect lower on new data.
Limits & risks
Trained on synthetic history. Little data for new products; one-off events (strikes, visa delays) are not in the data. Bias towards popular items — low-data products are flagged “Low data”.
Mitigation
Human approval, ranges not single numbers, retrain button, back-test shown below.

Recommendations (“Frequently bought together”)

Method
Item-to-item association rules (market-basket analysis) on anonymised order lines. For each pair A→B: support, confidence = P(B | A) and lift = confidence ÷ P(B). Only pairs seen in at least {{ rec_run.params.min_pair_count }} orders with lift > 1 are kept. Score = confidence × √lift.
Where it is used
Home page, product page and basket, always labelled “Smart suggestion” with a “How suggestions work” link.
Data
{{ rec_run.data_summary.baskets }} baskets, {{ rec_run.data_summary.rules }} rules. Bundle contents are excluded so the model does not just learn our own bundles.
Evaluation
Time-based split: train on the oldest 80% of orders, test on the newest 20% ({{ rec_run.metrics.test_baskets }} baskets). Hide one item from each basket and check whether it is in the top 3. Hit rate@3: {% widthratio rec_run.metrics.hit_rate_at_3 1 100 %}% vs {% widthratio rec_run.metrics.baseline_hit_rate_at_3 1 100 %}% for “most popular items”.
Safeguards
Out-of-stock and hidden items are never recommended. No personal data is used. Suggestions never change prices.
Limits
New products have no co-purchase history (cold start) — we fall back to popular items. Popular items can dominate (popularity bias).
